# **AI Engineers:** 

# **Tech Talk Series**

# **Presentation Criteria**

## **Format**

* **10-Minute Presentation:** Share something you’ve worked on or are actively working on in AI engineering.  
* **5-minute Q\&A:** Be ready for thoughtful follow-ups from an audience of peers and technical leaders.

## **What to Cover in Your 10 Minutes**

Use this as a prompt, not a prescription. Every talk is different, and we welcome creative structure.

1. **Context Setting**  
   * What was the goal?  
   * What was the core technical or product challenge?  
   * Why did it matter? (Impact on users, performance, scalability, etc.)  
2. **Approach & Tradeoffs**  
   * How did you approach it?  
   * What alternatives did you consider?  
3. **The Build**  
   * What did you actually build?  
   * Any modeling, architecture, infrastructure, or tooling worth highlighting?  
   * How did you balance speed, scale, and complexity?  
4. **Outcome**  
   * What worked? What didn’t?  
   * Any metrics, anecdotes, or learnings you can share?  
   * Would you do anything differently in hindsight?  
5. **Takeaway**  
   * What’s one thing you hope the audience walks away thinking about?  
   * What was the most surprising thing you learned?
